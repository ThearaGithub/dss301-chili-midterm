Two ways to get the data: A) open the NASA website with everything pre-selected and click Download there, or B) download the exact CSV files directly.

A. Open on the NASA website, pre-selected — then click Download there

CLICK HERE: NASA POWER viewer — the field, hourly, 04/07/2026 → 12/10/2026, Agroclimatology
  1. This opens the Single Point pane with the coordinates 12.141 / 104.085, Hourly, parameter T2M and the dates already filled in.
  2. In the parameter list tick the ones you need: T2M, T10M, RH2M, QV2M, QV10M, PRECTOTCORR, TSOIL1–6, GWETTOP, GWETROOT, GWETPROF.
  3. Click Submit → you will see the chart and the data table right on the site.
  4. Click Download (CSV) in that pane — done, you have it yourself.
